The question behind the map.

This project examines forest, cropland, and built-up land in Blora Regency, Central Java. Historical maps from 2004 to 2024 provide a basis for exploring a possible land-use scenario for 2034.

How I approached it.

Supervised land-cover classification was carried out in Google Earth Engine. QGIS was used to prepare slope and proximity inputs for roads, rivers, and built-up areas. MOLUSCE was used to model the 2034 scenario, followed by carbon storage estimation in InVEST using referenced carbon pool values.

What the work shows.

The outputs include historical land-cover comparisons, a modelled 2034 land-use map, and comparisons of estimated carbon stocks. The original analysis also explores a monetary illustration of carbon loss using a reference carbon price. That illustration should be treated as a scenario calculation rather than a project valuation.

Reading the results

Personal project. The 2034 map is a modelled scenario, not an observed outcome. Carbon stock estimates depend on land-cover classification and carbon pool assumptions.
