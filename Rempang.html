The question behind the map.

This project compares mangrove extent on Rempang Island in the Riau Islands between 2019 and 2024. The maps bring the two periods together to show the distribution of mangroves and areas of mapped change.

How I approached it.

Sentinel-2 satellite imagery was processed using supervised land-cover classification. Mangrove extent was compared between the two years, with NDVI imagery and QGIS outputs providing supporting spatial context.

What the work shows.

The project reports 224.08 hectares of mapped mangrove loss over the five-year period. The reported classification validation achieved 95.8% overall accuracy and a kappa value of 0.95. The gallery includes the change map, temporal comparisons, vegetation index imagery, and the GIS workflow.

Reading the results

Personal project. Classification accuracy and change estimates should be read alongside the validation approach. Satellite mapping alone does not establish the causes of mangrove loss.
